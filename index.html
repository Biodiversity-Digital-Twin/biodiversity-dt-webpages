About the Project

We are designing and developing an Earth System Digital Twin (ESDT) to represent and forecast carbon storage dynamics and habitat connectivity across Central Africa. This innovative system integrates models that quantify and predict the impacts of key environmental stressors including climate change, land-use change, and biodiversity loss.

The ESDT will provide a comprehensive digital representation and near-term forecasts of tropical forest structure, biomass, and biodiversity corridors across Central Africa, supporting the scientific objectives of PANGEA, a new terrestrial ecology field campaign.

Our system addresses three critical questions: What Then (past state), What Now (current state), and What Next (future forecasts) — providing stakeholders with actionable insights for conservation and policy decisions.
