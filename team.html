Project Team

Interdisciplinary collaboration across leading institutions

Team Overview

Our interdisciplinary team brings together expertise in remote sensing, ecology, computer science, and policy to develop this comprehensive Earth System Digital Twin. The team is organized into science and technology groups, each contributing specialized knowledge and capabilities.
